TRADECALC PRO — INDEPENDENT ARTICLE ADS + AFFILIATE SYSTEM

IMPORTANT: Article monetization is now separate from the homepage/calculator monetization.

DASHBOARD SECTIONS
1. Articles: create/edit/publish articles. Choose article-only ad positions 1–6 and select multiple article-only partner offers.
2. Article ads: configure up to six independent Monetag placements. These settings do not read or modify the existing homepage/calculator Monetag settings.
3. Article affiliate offers: add an independent catalogue of article partner links, including name, category, HTTPS affiliate URL, description, CTA, badge, country allow/block lists, priority, enable/disable and delete.

PUBLIC ARTICLE PAGE
- Selected article ads are distributed between article paragraphs and remaining placements appear after the article body.
- Article affiliate partners appear in a professional multi-card recommendation panel. Select four or five or more per article when suitable.
- Affiliate cards include a disclosure. Country allow/block rules are applied.
- Homepage calculator ads and calculator affiliate offers remain unchanged and are not reused by article pages.

INSTALL
1. Run article-monetization-independent-update.sql once in Supabase SQL Editor.
2. Upload all ZIP files to the existing GitHub repository and replace matching files.
3. Keep supabase-config.js unchanged.
4. Deploy, open Admin Dashboard, and use Article ads / Article affiliate offers to configure the separate systems.
5. Create or edit an article and select the ad positions and partner offers for that article.

MONETAG NOTES
Use unique zone tags from your Monetag publisher account. Network fill, format compatibility, visitor eligibility and consent settings are controlled by Monetag; code placement cannot guarantee an ad impression.
