Estimate your trading fees

This trading fee calculator estimates the fee for a trade using the asset price, quantity, fee percentage and number of trade sides. It can help estimate a simple buy-and-sell fee total when you enter two sides.

How this calculator works

The result is based on the fee rate you provide and the entered notional amount. Actual maker or taker tiers, discounts, minimum fees, spread, funding, conversion costs and taxes may not be included. Check the fee schedule for your specific exchange or broker.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a crypto trading fee?

Multiply the trade notional value by the fee percentage. For a round trip, calculate the fee for both buy and sell sides.

Does this know my exchange fee tier?

No. Enter the fee rate from your exchange or broker. The tool does not connect to exchange accounts or fetch personal fee tiers.
