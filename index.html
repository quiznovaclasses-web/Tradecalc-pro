Helpful information

Frequently asked questions about online trading calculators

Are TradeCalc Pro calculators free to use?

Yes. The calculator collection is available to use in your web browser without a paid subscription. Some advanced market calculations depend on the figures and assumptions you enter.

Do I need to connect my exchange or broker account?

No exchange or broker login is required to use the browser-based calculators. Enter the relevant values manually and review the result on screen.

Can I use the calculators on a mobile phone?

The website is designed for modern mobile and desktop browsers. For the best experience, use an up-to-date browser with JavaScript enabled.

Are calculator results live market prices or trading signals?

No. These tools calculate estimates from the inputs you provide; they are not a live price feed, investment recommendation or guaranteed trading signal. Confirm current prices and product specifications with your provider.

Why can my broker or exchange show a different result?

Platforms may use different contract sizes, tick values, fee tiers, spreads, funding rules, currency conversions, rounding methods and liquidation formulas. Compare the assumptions and verify the final figures with your provider.

About the platform

About TradeCalc Pro

TradeCalc Pro is an independent online calculation toolkit built to help people understand common numbers used in cryptocurrency, forex, stock trading and long-term investing. It brings together free browser-based tools for profit and loss, pip value, position sizing, leverage, margin, fees, risk management, portfolio analysis, dividends and investment returns.

The purpose of the platform is to make financial calculations easier to explore and check—not to tell anyone what to buy or sell. Enter your own assumptions, compare scenarios and learn how changes in price, quantity, fees or risk settings can affect an estimated result. No exchange account connection is needed for the calculators.

TradeCalc Pro does not provide personalized financial, investment, tax or legal advice. Calculator outputs may not include every charge or rule that applies to your product. Confirm live prices, contract specifications, spreads, funding rates, fees, tax treatment and local regulations with the relevant provider or a qualified professional. Trading and leveraged products involve substantial risk and can lead to losses.
